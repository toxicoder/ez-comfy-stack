    Cable descend¶

    What's on this page

    • A muted 5s illustration of Cable descend (move_cable_descend)
    • Catalog clause and still/motion/AV flags on Camera Movement

    What this enables

    • Seeing the pick before splicing it on Cinema Rack
    • Copying the clause next to a concrete camera example

    Do not hand-edit this file. Re-run python3 docs/generate_cinema_docs.py. Axis: Camera Movement. Playbook: Cinema Rack.

    Clause¶

    The camera descends along a cable toward a crowd, faces rising to meet a falling overhead lens.

    Use on¶

    still, motion, av
