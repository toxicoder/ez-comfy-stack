# Troubleshooting — models and workflows

**What's on this page**

- **Empty models**, bad CLIP/VAE links, MagCache, and App Mode
- **90s film** concat, dream-house, ACE/rap, and Enhance GGUF
- **Occupancy** blender-desk vs heavy klein / wan / ltx / trellis

**What this enables**

- **Fixing** Missing Models and graph-specific Queue failures
- **Keeping** MiniMax H3 out of `${MODELS_DIR}` and 90s films on 18 × 5.00 s shots

!!! danger "Do not Queue a 90s latent"

    Film graphs are 18 × **5.00 s** (121 frames, `1+8n`) + stitch. MiniMax H3 is banned — [Model licenses](../licenses.md). Occupancy XOR: [Troubleshooting — studio canvas](troubleshooting-canvas.md).
